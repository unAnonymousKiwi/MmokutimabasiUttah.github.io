Orange Farm Technologies

A smart farm that needed buyers, investors and trainees under one roof. I took its website from blank page to live in one month.

SEO and Content Lead. Website build, client project, 2026.

The Context

Orange Farm Tech grows vegetables and cash crops with smart farming in Abia State. The team wanted to reach beyond Eastern Nigeria.

The website also had extra jobs: a portfolio for investors and a training ground for people who want to learn.

The Core Problem

One site had three audiences and one deadline. Buyers wanted fresh produce quickly, investors wanted proof, and trainees wanted to learn. Smart farming also speaks a technical language that ordinary shoppers do not.

The Struggle

A month is a tight window for a full build. The brief also evolved many times as the team’s vision matured. My job was to keep the work steady and earn their trust in the process.

The Execution

  1. Start in the field. I study crop protection and environmental biology, so I sat down with a couple of my professors and with the agronomists on the farm. They walked me through the tools, the equipment and the process. Call it home advantage.
  2. Think like the shopper. I also considered everyday users who just want to buy vegetables fast. A clear route to buy fresh produce sits at the top of the home page.
  3. Plan the pages. I planned the structure and created every core page, with search intent, keywords and on-page SEO built in from the first line. The site covers home, about, blog, contact, careers and community.
  4. Build and launch. I built and launched the site in Framer.

Tools: Framer, keyword research and on-page SEO, plus interviews with professors and agronomists.

The Win

A blank page became a live, search-ready website in one month.

Lighthouse scored SEO at 100 on desktop and 92 on mobile, with best practices at 100 on both. Desktop performance scored 90. Mobile performance scored 71, the one number still worth improving.
